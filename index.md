<p class="lead">I am a postdoctoral researcher at <strong>ENS Paris</strong>, in the DATA team of the <a href="https://csd.ens.psl.eu/">Centre for Data Science</a>, where I work with <a href="https://brloureiro.github.io/">Bruno Loureiro</a>.</p>

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at **École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)**, working with [Florent Krzakala](https://florentkrzakala.com/). Prior to that, I completed my PhD under the supervision of [Satya Majumdar](https://www.lptms.universite-paris-saclay.fr/satya-majumdar/) and [Marc Potters](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marc-potters-cfm/). I hold Master's degrees in **Mathematics** and **Theoretical Physics** from **ENS Paris** and **Sorbonne Université**, respectively.

My research investigates problems in **machine learning**, **high-dimensional statistics and probability**, and **statistical physics**, especially when **random matrix theory** can be used to characterize statistical limits, phase transitions, and algorithmic performance.

Current themes include spectral methods for high-dimensional inference, feature learning, multi-modal learning, non-linear low-rank estimation, and random matrix/free-probability questions.
